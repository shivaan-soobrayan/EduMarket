Why Use CampusRide?

Affordable

Share fuel costs with fellow Eduvos students instead of paying expensive transport fees.

Student Only

CampusRide is designed specifically for the Eduvos student community.

Convenient

Find rides based on your route, date and departure time.

Connected

Connect directly with students who are travelling in the same direction.

How CampusRide Works

1

Find a Ride

Search for students travelling along your route.

2

Request

Send a request to the driver with your contact details.

3

Get Accepted

The driver reviews your request and accepts or declines it.

4

Travel

Meet at the agreed pickup location and travel together.

CampusRide Safety

Always verify that the person you are travelling with is an Eduvos student. Meet in safe public locations and never share sensitive financial information.
